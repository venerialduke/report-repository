Referred Pine+ members churned 7.1% in month 3, vs. 16.2% for paid social

8-week referral pilot readout: the pilot beat its sign-up target by 38% at $21 per sign-up, and we recommend rolling it out to all members in October.

By Daniel Reyes Published Sep 24, 2026 Pilot 8 weeks, readout Aug 2026

The short version

  1. The pilot beat its sign-up target by 38%: 4,140 referred sign-ups against a target of 3,000, at $21 per sign-up.
  2. Referred members churn less in months 2–4. In month 3, 7.1% of referred members churned against 16.2% of paid-social members (p < 0.01).
  3. Referral is the cheapest channel we tested, at $21 per sign-up against $27 for search and $34 for paid social.
  4. Recommendation: extend the referral credit to all Pine+ members from Oct 6, keep a 10% holdout to measure incrementality, and revisit the $15 credit after 12 weeks.

01The pilot beat its sign-up target by 38%

Referrals brought in 4,140 new members in eight weeks, against a target of 3,000 set in the Q2 plan.

Each sign-up cost $21. That includes the $15 referral credit and the operational time to run the pilot.

02Referred members churn less in months 2–4, and the gap is widest in month 3

In month 3, 7.1% of referred members churned, compared with 16.2% of members acquired through paid social. That gap is statistically significant (p < 0.01).
